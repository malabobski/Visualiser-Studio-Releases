# Visualiser Studio

**A place to hang out with your music.**

A music visualiser for Windows and macOS. Turn your tracks and live audio into reactive visuals, build your own stage, and share your favourite looks.

[Download Visualiser Studio](https://malchim.malabobski.workers.dev/) · [Latest release](https://github.com/malabobski/Visualiser-Studio-Releases/releases/latest) · [Community](https://malchim.malabobski.workers.dev/#community)

## Make the music yours

- **Live audio:** visualise desktop audio as you listen.
- **Create:** turn audio tracks into visualiser videos with custom colours, automation and song sections.
- **MIDI visuals:** attach MIDI files and customise how notes move and react.
- **Stage Designer:** add text, images and artwork to your stage.
- **Miniplayer:** keep your music controls close while you work.
- **Community:** find friends, send messages and share saved templates.

## Downloads

The [website](https://malchim.malabobski.workers.dev/) helps you choose the download for your system. You can also find installers and change notes on the [latest release page](https://github.com/malabobski/Visualiser-Studio-Releases/releases/latest).

| System | Download |
| --- | --- |
| Windows | The Setup `.exe` installer |
| Mac with Apple silicon | The `mac-arm64.dmg` installer |
| Mac with an Intel processor | The `mac-x64.dmg` installer |

On a Mac, open **Apple menu → About This Mac** to check whether it lists an Apple chip or an Intel processor.

Installed Windows apps can check for updates and let you choose when to restart. Mac updates currently use a manual download.
